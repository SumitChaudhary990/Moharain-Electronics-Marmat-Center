📊 End-to-End Accounting Flow (Example: Sales / Repair Invoice Lifecycle)

1
Presentation Layer: User completes sales counter bill or registers repair ticket in Frontend UI.
2
Application Layer: Backend / Logic validates prices, deducts inventory stock (COGS), and posts due balances to Accounts Receivable (AR).
3
General Ledger: Double-entry journal transaction is recorded: Debit Cash / AR, Credit Sales Revenue, Debit COGS, Credit Inventory Asset.
4
Data & Security Layer: Entry is stored in persistent storage with immutable staff audit trail (staffName) and Nepal time.
5
Reporting Module: Real-time dashboard, Profit & Loss (P&L), and Balance Sheet are updated instantly!
